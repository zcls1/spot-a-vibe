Welcome to Spot-a-Vibe!

About

Spot-a-Vibe guesses your music taste from your style. Step in front of the camera and Cloudflare's Clef-Flash model looks at your outfit and accessories to pick the genre that fits you, then narrows it down to a sub-genre, like Trap, Emo or Bebop. Connect Spotify and every vibe comes with songs from well-known artists in that style, ready to save as a playlist.

Getting Started

  1. Step 1

    Press Start Here!, then Start camera and allow camera access. Center yourself in the frame, with only ONE person in the frame at a time.

  2. Step 2

    Once the camera recognizes you, it will start a countdown to take a picture of you. Press Capture to take one right away.

  3. Step 3

    After a few seconds, your genre, sub-genre and playlist will be revealed! Connect Spotify to get songs and save them.

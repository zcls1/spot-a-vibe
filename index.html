Welcome to Spot-a-Vibe!

About

Spot-a-Vibe guesses your music taste from your style. Step in front of the camera and Cloudflare's Clef-Flash model looks at your outfit and accessories to pick the genre that fits you, then narrows it down to a sub-genre, like Trap, Emo or Bebop. Connect Spotify and every vibe comes with songs from well-known artists in that style, ready to save as a playlist.

Getting Started

  1. Step 1

    Press Start Here!, then Start camera and allow camera access. Connect Spotify too if you want songs for your vibe.

    1

  2. Step 2

    Center yourself in front of the camera. A photo is taken after a 3-second countdown, or press Capture to take one right away.

    2

  3. Step 3

    See your vibe: your genre, your sub-genre and songs to match. Save them as a playlist, or scan again.

    3
